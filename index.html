Overview

In the attendees page you will be able to input attendee information like their name, email-address, phone number, age, and affiliation.

In the schedules page you will be able to manage the events within your conference. Listing such as type, date, and length of time.

Material management will let you list and keep track of all materials needed for your conference. Such as speakers, chairs, dividers, etc.
