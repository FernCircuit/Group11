Overview

The attendees page helps you be able to input attendee information like their name, email-address, phone number, age, and affiliation. Having these details in one place makes it easier to review attendee requests, communicate with participants, and prepare for a successful conference experience.

The Schedule section helps you plan and coordinate events taking place during your conference. Plan and organize your events. Manage Conference events, dates, times and schedule. Keeping event details organized makes it easier for organizers to manage their plans, coordinate different activites and ensure that attendes know what to expect throughout the event.

The Material Management section helps you keep track of the resources and supplies needed to support your conference. Organziers can use this section to plan for supplies they will need such as chairs, room dividers, mics, etc. Maintaining an organized list of materials that will be needed can help prevent last minute problems and ensure that the conference space is fully prepared for speakers, staff and audience/attendees.
